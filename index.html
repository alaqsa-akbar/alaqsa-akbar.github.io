    Research

    SSL-DiT: Saudi Sign Language video generation using multi-condition diffusion transformers

    My undergraduate thesis. A diffusion transformer generates Saudi Sign Language video from several conditioning signals at once.

    It grew out of my proposal, SSLVG, a text-to-gloss-to-video pipeline: each sign's pose is retrieved as a VQ-VAE latent, a transformer models the transitions between signs, and the result conditions a DiT. Evaluated on Isharah-1000 and KArSL.

    KFUPM, 2026. Advised by Dr. Motaz Alfarraj. Paper in preparation.

    BreachSeek: a multi-agent automated penetration tester

    Language-model agents that plan a penetration test, carry out the attack, and report the vulnerabilities they find.

    Alshehri, Alshehri, Almalki, Bamardouf and Akbar. arXiv, 2024.
